Record key and how the downloads work

Record key: AIG-INV-04 is the Register and owns one permanent AIR-ID and current assurance state per system; use cases require their own exact UC-ID scope and may share that AIR-ID. AIG-DEC-04 is the separate Gate Log for plans, events and conditions. A system-level Approved baseline does not approve any UC-ID, and unknown use scope is not shared scope. Proposed controlled AIG-INV-05 is a relationship map only—not an approved/adopted record, decision, permission or approval source. Do not invent an AIR-ID, event ID, authority, approval, legal conclusion, FRIA completion, publication or ISO conformity. Each download uses the exact column headers, order and controlled lists of its AI governance suite v3.9.8 target (guidance columns sit after a blank spacer and are never pasted); still verify every value against the current controlled record before transfer.

AGPI is prioritisation, not a waiver. Equality Act 2010 section 149, Human Rights Act 1998 section 6, privacy and other case-specific duties require screening for every tier. EU AI Act, ATRS and procurement duties are conditional: ask the legal/procurement owner to confirm applicability for the case.

Prospective Gate Plan — separate from a decision

A plan is prospective scheduling only. It is not an approval, a Gate Event, nor evidence that an assurance condition is met. Provide its exact UC-ID or explicitly mark a shared system baseline; unknown scope is not shared scope. Provide the current workbook source version; the Council assigns Plan ID.

Dated Gate Event transfer checklist — only after a real authorised decision

This is a transfer checklist for an actual verified authorised event, not an authoritative event record. The formal decision remains in AIG-DEC-03 or authorised native forum minutes. AIG-DEC-04 should point to that record; AIG-AGT-04 governs agent authority. Event type, Outcome, Gate / forum and lifecycle stage use the AIG-DEC-04 v1.2 draft controlled lists. A system-baseline Approved state never approves a UC-ID. No operational-use claim is supported without a per-UC decision reference and its exact permitted scope and conditions; this draft creates no decision, permission, event ID or approval.
